Account Deletion Request

Institution of Municipal Engineers (India)

IME India respects your privacy and gives members the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal information.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Send an email using your registered email address.
  2. Use the subject line "Account Deletion Request".
  3. Include the following details:
    • Full Name
    • Membership Number
    • Registered Email Address
    • Registered Mobile Number

Verification Process

To protect your account from unauthorized deletion, IME India may verify your identity before processing your request.

How Your Account is Deleted

After your identity is verified, an authorized IME India administrator will permanently delete your account using the Member Management system in the IME Admin Dashboard.

Processing Time

Most deletion requests are completed within 7 working days after successful verification.

Information That Will Be Deleted

  • Member account
  • Login credentials
  • Profile information
  • Profile photo
  • User-generated posts and comments (or they may be anonymized if required to preserve discussion history)

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required by applicable laws or legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Membership transaction history
  • Financial records
  • Records required for legal or regulatory compliance
  • System backup logs for a limited period
